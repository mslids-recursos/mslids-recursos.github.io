Ms. Lids · How to play

How to play 👻

  1. Pass the phone. Each player looks at their secret word alone. Don't show anyone!
  2. One player is the ghost. The ghost doesn't know the word.
  3. Give clues. In order, each player says ONE clue in English. Don't say the word!
  4. The ghost listens and pretends to know the word. 🤫
  5. After 1–2 rounds, everyone votes: count to 3 and point at the ghost.
  6. If the ghost is caught, it gets one chance to guess the word. Correct = the ghost wins!
Clue starters
  • It's something you can…
  • You can find it in / at…
  • It's usually… (color / size)
  • People use it to…
  • It reminds me of…
